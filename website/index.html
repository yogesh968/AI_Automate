Install

Download for your OS and run the installer. On Windows, click “More info → Run anyway” if SmartScreen asks. On Mac, allow Microphone, Accessibility and Screen Recording when prompted.

Add your keys

Open Settings and paste a free Groq key. Optionally add an ElevenLabs key and a Hindi voice.

Say “Hey Jarvis”

That's it. Use Ctrl+Alt+J (⌘⌥J on Mac) any time to stop everything instantly.

Questions

 

Is it really free?

Yes. Jarvis is free and open source. The AI runs on Groq's free tier with your own key; ElevenLabs is optional, and without it Jarvis uses free Microsoft voices.

Is it always listening to me?

Only for the wake word, and that runs on your computer. Audio is sent for transcription only after “Hey Jarvis” or when you press the talk shortcut. You can mute the mic any time from the menu bar or tray.

Which Mac do I need?

macOS 12 or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel. Grant Microphone, Accessibility and Screen Recording so it can hear you, control apps and see the screen when you ask.

It feels slow. What can I do?

Add an ElevenLabs key and pick the Flash voice model, keep “Instant commands” on in Settings, and use short commands for everyday things. On the free Groq tier, very long requests can hit the per-minute limit; Jarvis switches models automatically when that happens.

Can it do something dangerous by mistake?

Anything that deletes, sends, pays or runs a command waits for your “haan”. System folders and destructive commands are blocked outright, and there's a dry-run mode to test routines safely.
