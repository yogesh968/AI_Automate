Does real work

Jarvis doesn't just chat. It has 88 tools that act on your computer.

🎙️

Voice, Hinglish first

Offline “Hey Jarvis” wake word, Whisper speech-to-text, and a natural ElevenLabs voice that mixes Hindi and English the way you do.

🖥️

Full device control

Apps, windows, volume, brightness, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, dark mode, power, clipboard, keyboard and mouse.

👁️

Sees your screen

“Screen pe jo error hai woh samjhao.” It reads, explains and clicks things by description.

📁

Files

Search, read PDFs and Word docs, summarize, organize Downloads by type, move, copy, and delete to the Recycle Bin.

🌐

Web & browser

Search, news, weather, read any page, and drive its own browser to click and fill forms. YouTube, Spotify and WhatsApp shortcuts.

📧

Email & calendar

Gmail and Google Calendar: read, reply, schedule. On Mac it also works with Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Music and iMessage.

🧠

Remembers you

Your people, preferences and “usual setup”. It uses what it knows in every conversation.

⏰

Reminders & routines

Timers, repeating reminders, and routines like “good morning” and “work mode”, or your own.

The orb

A floating Three.js orb that pulses, listens, thinks and speaks. Click it for a glass chat panel. It never gets in your way.

Powerful, but safe

Every action goes through a safety layer, and you stay in control.

Up in 3 minutes

 

Install

Download for your OS and run the installer. On Windows, click “More info → Run anyway” if SmartScreen asks. On Mac, allow Microphone, Accessibility and Screen Recording when prompted.

Add your keys

Click the orb, open Settings, and paste a free Groq key. Optionally add an ElevenLabs key and a Hindi voice.

Say “Hey Jarvis”

That's it. Use Ctrl+Alt+J (⌘⌥J on Mac) any time to stop everything instantly.
